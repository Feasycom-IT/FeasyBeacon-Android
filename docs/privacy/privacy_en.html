FeasyBeacon Privacy Policy

Version V1　Updated: November 19, 2026　Effective: November 19, 2026

Shenzhen Feasycom Technology Co., Ltd. ("we", "us") understands the importance of your personal information and is committed to protecting it. This Privacy Policy explains how FeasyBeacon (package name: com.feasycom.feasybeacon, the "App") collects, uses and protects your information, and the rights you have.

Please read this Policy carefully before using the App. Key clauses are shown in bold. By tapping "Agree" you confirm that you have read and agree to this Policy. If you do not agree, please do not tap "Agree" — we will not collect any personal information and you may exit the App.

1. Information We Collect and Use

We follow the principle of minimum necessity and only collect what is required for the features below. The core function of the App is to discover, connect to and configure Bluetooth Low Energy (BLE) beacons, which requires Bluetooth and location permissions.

What we do NOT collect

The App does not collect: IMEI, IMSI, OAID, device MAC address, phone number, contacts, SMS/MMS, call logs, photos, installed app list, clipboard content, location history, or biometric data.
The clipboard is only written (when you tap "Copy error info" on the crash screen); it is never read.

3. No Collection Before Consent

Before you tap "Agree":
  • We do not initialize any third-party SDK that collects personal information;
  • We do not make any network request (model database, ad configuration, update check);
  • We do not read IMEI, IMSI, OAID, device MAC, app list or clipboard;
  • We do not request Bluetooth, location or camera permissions.
These features start only after you tap "Agree".

5. Storage and Protection

  • Location: device records, QR images, firmware files and cached ads stay on your device; network requests use HTTPS.
  • Retention: local data is deleted when you clear app data or uninstall the App.
  • Security: we apply access control and encrypted transmission; however, no Internet environment is absolutely secure.
  • Exceptions: we may provide information where required by law or competent authorities.

6. Sharing, Transfer and Disclosure

  • Sharing: apart from the SDKs listed in Section 2, we do not share your personal information with third parties.
  • Transfer: no transfer, except in the event of a merger or restructuring, where the recipient remains bound by this Policy.
  • Disclosure: no public disclosure, unless required by law or with your separate consent.
  • Cross-border: we do not transfer your personal information outside your country/region.

7. Your Rights

  • Access and correction: you can view device records and parameters in the App; contact us for correction.
  • Deletion: delete device records in the App, or clear app data / uninstall the App to remove all local data.
  • Withdraw consent: clear app data in system Settings → Apps → FeasyBeacon → Storage, or uninstall the App. After withdrawal we will not initialize third-party SDKs or make network requests. Withdrawal does not affect processing carried out before withdrawal.
  • Account cancellation: the App has no account registration, so no account deletion is needed.
  • Complaints: contact us via the details below; we respond within 15 business days.

8. Minors

The App is mainly intended for business users and technicians. If you are under 14, please read this Policy with your guardian and use the App only with your guardian's consent. We do not knowingly collect minors' personal information and will delete it if discovered.

9. Updates to This Policy

We may update this Policy. After an update we will ask for your consent again via a dialog at startup; for material changes (purpose, data types, sharing) we will notify you more prominently. The version number is shown under the title.
