3. Services

3.1 The App is a local configuration tool for Bluetooth Low Energy devices (beacons and sensors). 3.2 Most features work locally on your phone without a cloud account. Some features require Internet access: updating the device model database, checking for new versions, downloading firmware, loading the launch-page advertisement, and browsing the product page (an embedded website that may use cookies and access logs).
